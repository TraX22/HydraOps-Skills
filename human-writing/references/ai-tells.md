# AI tells, by category

None of these is forbidden in isolation. The problem is density and predictability.
When you see several in one paragraph, rewrite the paragraph.

## Openers

| Tell | Instead |
|---|---|
| In today's fast-paced / digital / ever-changing world | Start with the specific claim or situation |
| In the realm of / landscape of / world of X | Name X directly |
| Let's dive in / Let's delve into / Let's explore | Just begin |
| Great question! / Certainly! / Absolutely! | Answer |
| I hope this email finds you well | A line that relates to the reader, or nothing |
| As an expert in X, ... | Show expertise through the content |

## Vocabulary (English)

delve, tapestry, testament, realm, landscape, navigate (non-literal), leverage (verb),
harness, unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, foster, bolster, streamline, seamless,
robust, cutting-edge, game-changer, pivotal, crucial, paramount, myriad, plethora,
multifaceted, nuanced (as filler), intricate, meticulous, comprehensive, holistic,
synergy, embark on a journey, a testament to, plays a vital role, serves as, underscores,
stands as, it is worth noting, notably, moreover, furthermore, additionally (at sentence
starts, repeatedly).

## Equivalent tells in other languages (examples)

- **Spanish**: "En el mundo actual", "En la era digital", "sumergirnos", "un sinfín de",
  "desempeña un papel crucial", "cabe destacar que", "en definitiva", "es importante
  señalar", "potenciar", "sin lugar a dudas", "adentrémonos", overuse of "además" and
  "asimismo" as sentence starters, anglicized punctuation (Title Case In Headings, colon
  before every list).
- **Portuguese**: "No mundo atual", "desempenha um papel fundamental", "vale ressaltar",
  "mergulhar", "em suma", "potencializar".
- **French**: "Dans le monde d'aujourd'hui", "il est important de noter", "plonger dans",
  "en somme", "jouer un rôle crucial", "véritable" as filler.
- **Italian**: "Nel mondo di oggi", "è importante sottolineare", "immergersi",
  "svolge un ruolo cruciale", "in conclusione".

## Structures

- "It's not just X, it's Y." / "No es solo X, es Y."
- "Whether you're a beginner or an expert, ..."
- "From A to B, X does it all."
- "X isn't about Y. It's about Z."
- Rhetorical question followed by its answer, every section.
- Three adjectives or three parallel clauses in most sentences.
- Every paragraph ends with a one-sentence mini-summary.
- A colon-led reveal: "The result? Pure magic."

## Punctuation and formatting

- Em dashes used as the default connector.
- Title Case Headings in languages that do not use them.
- Bold on key phrases in every paragraph.
- Bullets with a bolded label and a colon, for content that is really a narrative.
- Emoji as bullet points or section headers in professional text.
- A "Key takeaways" or "Conclusion" section on a short piece.

## Tone

- Relentless positivity; no drawbacks mentioned.
- Balanced to the point of saying nothing ("there are pros and cons").
- Hedged claims: "can potentially help to improve".
- Praise for the reader or the question.
- Moralizing add-ons nobody asked for ("Remember, it's important to always...").

## Endings

- "In conclusion," / "Ultimately," / "In summary," / "At the end of the day,"
- "By following these steps, you'll be well on your way to..."
- "The possibilities are endless." / "The future is bright."
- Offering more help in a piece meant to be published.
