# Before and after

The "after" versions use concrete details (names, places, numbers) as the author would
supply them. In real work you do not invent those details: take them from the user's
material, ask for them, or leave a placeholder such as `[name]` or `[x]%`.

## 1. Product update email (English)

**Before**
> In today's fast-paced digital landscape, staying organized is more crucial than ever.
> That's why we're thrilled to announce our latest feature — Smart Folders — a
> game-changing tool designed to streamline your workflow, boost productivity, and
> elevate your experience. Whether you're a freelancer or a large team, Smart Folders
> seamlessly adapts to your needs. In conclusion, we can't wait for you to try it!

**After**
> Smart Folders are live. Set a rule once (say, "invoices from this client") and matching
> files file themselves. If you have ever lost ten minutes hunting for last month's
> receipt, this is for you. It's on for everyone today; you'll find it in the sidebar.

What changed: removed opener and closer, cut the triad, removed the em dash, replaced
"streamline your workflow" with what the feature actually does, added one concrete
scenario.
