# Instagram

Instagram is visual first and relationship driven. Reels reach new people; carousels get
saved and shared; Stories and DMs keep existing followers close. Shares (especially via
DM) and saves are strong signals that a post is worth showing to more people.

## Current facts (as of September 2026 - re-check with `web_search` if a number matters)

- Hashtags: **up to 5 per post or Reel** (Instagram announced the limit in December 2025).
- Caption: up to **2,200 characters**; roughly the first 125 characters show before
  "more".
- Reels: can be up to **20 minutes** long, but recommendations to non-followers favor
  short Reels (commonly reported: under about 3 minutes). 9:16, 1080x1920.
- Carousels: up to **20** images/videos in the app (third-party schedulers using the API
  may be limited to 10). Feed images 4:5 (1080x1350) take the most screen space.

## Audience and what performs

- Lifestyle, creators, local business, e-commerce, food, travel, fitness, design,
  education in bite-sized form.
- Performs: Reels with a strong first frame and a clear payoff; carousels that teach
  (step by step, mistakes, before/after, checklists); relatable or aspirational content;
  consistent visual identity; content people send to a friend.
- Does not: text-heavy single images, stock photos with captions, watermarked TikTok
  reposts, engagement pods, hashtag walls.

## Reels script

1. **Hook, 0-2 s**: strong first frame + on-screen text + spoken line. Movement or a
   result in frame one.
2. **Body**: one idea, quick cuts, each beat earns the next.
3. **Payoff**: deliver the promise.
4. **End**: loop, punchline, or a reason to share/save ("Send this to the friend who...").
- Text overlays inside safe zones (keep clear of the bottom UI and the right-side
  buttons); captions on for muted viewing.
- Cover image: choose a frame or custom cover that reads well in the grid (4:5 crop).

## Carousel

- **Slide 1**: the hook - a promise, a bold claim or a problem, big text, minimal words.
- **Slides 2-(n-1)**: one point per slide, consistent layout, readable in 3 seconds.
- **Last slide**: summary or CTA (save, share, follow, comment a keyword).
- 5-10 slides is a practical sweet spot; use more only if each slide adds something.
- The caption adds context, it does not repeat the slides.

## Caption

- First line: a hook or the key sentence (it is what shows before "more").
- Body: context, story or steps; short paragraphs.
- CTA: one clear ask.
- Up to 5 hashtags, specific over broad (e.g. `#sourdoughbeginner` beats `#food`).
- Keywords in the caption help Instagram search; write naturally.

## Stories

- Behind the scenes, polls, questions, quizzes, countdowns, links to new posts.
- 3-7 frames per sequence, one message; use stickers to invite taps and replies.
- Highlights for evergreen info (FAQ, prices, reviews).

## Cadence

- A sustainable baseline: 3-5 feed posts per week (mix of Reels and carousels) and Stories
  most days. Consistency beats volume.
- Reply to comments and DMs quickly; conversation feeds reach.

## Repurposing

- TikTok -> Reels: re-export without watermark, rewrite caption, 5 hashtags max.
- X thread or blog listicle -> carousel.
- YouTube long-form -> 2-4 Reels + one summary carousel.
- LinkedIn carousel (PDF) -> Instagram carousel with lighter tone and visuals.

## Avoid

- More than 5 hashtags (over the limit as of Sep 2026; generic tags also dilute topic signals).
- Buying followers, engagement pods, follow/unfollow.
- Undisclosed sponsorships (use the paid partnership label).
- Misleading before/after or health claims.
- Publishing anything without the user's explicit approval.
